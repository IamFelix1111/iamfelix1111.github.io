ExtendedHotbar

Place the bottom row of your inventory above the hotbar, doubling your quick-access slots.

Latest version: 1.0.2 for Minecraft 1.21.11.

Keybindings

  • R — Swap the hotbar with the row above it.
  • Shift+R — Swap only the items in your hand.
  • slot key — Press the currently selected hotbar slot's key again to swap just that slot.
  • = — Enable or disable the mod.
  • V — Toggle fluent mode.

All keybinds are configurable in Minecraft's "Controls" settings, under the "Extended Hotbar" category. Key actions are edge-triggered, so holding a key down won't repeat the action.

Fluent Mode

Fluent mode swaps transparently as you scroll along a double-wide hotbar, rendering the second row next to the first instead of on top. Enable it from the configuration screen (requires Mod Menu) or by pressing V.

While the secondary hotbar is selected, opening your inventory or any container (chest, furnace, etc.) shows your items in their original rows, and clicking or shift-clicking acts on the item you see. This works in single-player and on servers, in both survival and creative mode, including the creative inventory's item-grid tabs.

Requirements

  • Minecraft 1.21.11
  • Fabric Loader 0.19.3+
  • Fabric API
  • Java 21+

Optional: Mod Menu adds the in-game configuration screen. Cloth Config is bundled in the mod jar.
